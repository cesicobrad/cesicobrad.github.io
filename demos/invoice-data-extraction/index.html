Document extraction

Emails, PDFs and photos of invoices turned into structured data. Totals are checked in code and a person approves each record.

What it does

  • Reads forwarded emails, PDF invoices and orders, phone photos of paper invoices and notes from a phone call. No separate OCR step.
  • Fills a fixed set of fields: parties, document number, dates, currency, line items, discount, tax and total.
  • Checks the arithmetic in code: quantity times price, the line total, tax at the stated rate and the total. The checks re-run as you edit.
  • Flags anything a person should look at, like a stamp covering a price.
  • Nothing is saved until a person approves it, and the record notes whether they changed anything.

When things go wrong

  • A wrong total on the invoice is kept as printed and flagged, not silently corrected.
  • Handles European number formats and Slovenian invoices.
