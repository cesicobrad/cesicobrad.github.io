Demo

Bike workshop assistant

Answers prices and hours in three languages, hands repairs to staff with the photos, and sends quotes the customer can accept.

What it does

  • Answers price and opening-hours questions in English, Slovenian or Croatian/Serbian, only from facts the owner approved, and shows where the answer came from.
  • Hands anything custom to a person, with the whole conversation and up to three photos.
  • Staff write a quote, check the exact total and approve it. The customer can accept it or ask a question, and an expired or revised quote can't be accepted.
  • Every request has an owner, a next action and a due time. Overdue work raises an alert.
  • Questions it couldn't answer are collected for the owner, who writes the answer once and publishes it.
  • Three days after a reply with no answer, n8n drafts a follow-up for approval. A customer reply cancels it.

When things go wrong

  • A repeated form submission creates one enquiry, not two.
  • Two staff approving at the same moment send one message.
  • If sending fails, the approved message is kept and retried.
  • Conflicting prices go to a person instead of being guessed.
