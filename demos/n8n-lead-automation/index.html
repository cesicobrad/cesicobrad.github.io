Lead automation in n8n

Reads each enquiry and the sender's website, scores the lead hot, warm or cold with the reasons, and drafts a reply. Nothing is sent until a person approves it.

What it does

  • Takes enquiries from a website form or an inbox and sorts them by language, intent and urgency. Spam is logged and nobody is bothered.
  • Reads the sender's company website and reports what the business does.
  • Scores the lead from 0 to 100 with a reason for every point: hot, warm or cold. The model reports what it sees, and code decides the number, so the score is consistent.
  • Drafts a reply in the customer's language. Hot and warm leads get a link to book a call.
  • Sends the owner a Telegram message with the score, the reasons and a link to approve, edit or reject the reply.
  • Logs everything in the CRM, drafts a follow-up after three days without an answer, and sends a short summary every Monday.

When things go wrong

  • Every step retries on errors and has a fallback, so a broken website or an AI outage never loses an enquiry.
  • An approval link works once, so a double click can't send twice.
  • Failed runs send an alert.
